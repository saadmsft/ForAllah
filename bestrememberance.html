Exploring the prophetic wisdom on the most excellent forms of remembrance and supplication

The Prophetic Teaching

أَفْضَلُ الذِّكْرِ لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللَّهُ وَأَفْضَلُ الدُّعَاءِ الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ

"The best remembrance is 'La ilaha illa Allah' (There is no god but Allah), and the best supplication is 'Alhamdulillah' (All praise is for Allah)."

Narrated by Jabir ibn Abdullah (RA) - Recorded by Imam at-Tirmidhi

لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللَّهُ

The Best Remembrance

The Declaration of Tawhid

"La ilaha illa Allah" is the foundation of Islamic faith - the testimony of Divine Oneness. This phrase negates all false deities and affirms the absolute sovereignty of Allah alone. It is the key that opens the gates of Paradise and the essence of all worship.

Benefits of Regular Recitation:
  • • Purifies the heart from shirk (associating partners with Allah)
  • • Strengthens one's connection with Allah
  • • Brings peace and tranquility to the soul
  • • Protects from spiritual harm

The Power of Negation and Affirmation

The structure of this dhikr contains two powerful elements: "La ilaha" (there is no god) removes false beliefs from the heart, while "illa Allah" (except Allah) fills it with the light of true faith. This process purifies and elevates the believer's spiritual state.

"Whoever says 'La ilaha illa Allah' sincerely from their heart will enter Paradise."
- Prophet Muhammad ﷺ

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ

The Best Supplication

The Expression of Gratitude

"Alhamdulillah" encompasses all forms of praise and gratitude to Allah. It acknowledges Allah as the source of all blessings and recognizes His perfect attributes. This phrase is considered the best supplication because gratitude itself is a form of worship that invites more divine blessings.

لَئِن شَكَرْتُمْ لَأَزِيدَنَّكُمْ

"If you are grateful, I will surely increase you [in favor]" - Qur'an 14:7

The Gateway to More Blessings

Saying "Alhamdulillah" in response to Allah's blessings is itself a greater blessing than what prompted it. The Prophet ﷺ taught that when a servant says "Alhamdulillah" after receiving a favor, the reward for this gratitude is better than the worldly blessing itself.

Times to Remember:
  • • After eating and drinking
  • • Upon waking up
  • • During times of ease and difficulty
  • • As a constant state of the heart

Incorporating These Into Daily Life

Morning & Evening

Begin and end each day with these blessed phrases to maintain spiritual connection

With Presence of Heart

Recite with mindfulness and contemplation of their meanings for maximum spiritual benefit

Consistent Practice

Make these dhikr a regular habit, even if in small amounts, for lasting spiritual growth
